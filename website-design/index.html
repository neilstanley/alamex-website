Speed is what turns visitors into customers

Most of your visitors are on a phone, often on mobile data. If your page is still loading after a few seconds, they press back and call the next business on the list. A fast site keeps them.

Google notices too. Speed and mobile usability feed into where you rank, and if you run Google Ads, a fast landing page improves your landing page experience, which helps your ads win better positions for the same budget.

Most small business websites are slow because they are built on heavy themes, page builders and a pile of plugins. We hand-build lean sites instead, and measure the result before you pay for it.

A recent rebuild

A South African manufacturer supplying schools, clinics and communities.

Where they started: an outdated WordPress site on shared hosting, running a years-old version with a slider plugin and close to a gigabyte of files behind it.

What we built: a new, hand-built site designed for phones first, with the headline and both call-to-action buttons on the first screen. The result: 97 out of 100 for speed on Google's mobile test, 100 for SEO and 100 for accessibility, with no layout shift. Changes the client asks for go live the same week, checked on a phone first.

Website questions

The short answers. Get in touch for the longer ones.

How fast will my new website be?

We build to score 90 or more out of 100 on Google's mobile speed test (Lighthouse, the engine behind PageSpeed Insights), with the main content appearing in under three seconds on Google's simulated mid-range phone. Our most recent rebuild scores 97 for speed and 100 for SEO and accessibility. We test before launch and share the scores with you.

Will I lose my Google rankings if I change websites?

Not if the move is done properly. We map every address on your old site to its new home with permanent redirects, carry over titles and descriptions, and submit the new sitemap to Google. Nobody following an old link or a Google result lands on an error page.

Do you build websites for businesses in South Africa?

Yes. We work with businesses in South Africa and the UK. Sites are served from Cloudflare's network, which has data centres in Johannesburg and Cape Town, so pages load quickly for South African visitors on mobile data. We work by WhatsApp, email and video call.

Can you also bring visitors to the website?

Yes, that is the other half of what we do. We run SEO and Google Ads, so the site, the tracking and the marketing are set up by the same team and measured against the same thing: enquiries.

How do changes get made after launch?

You tell us what you want changed and we make it. Every change is checked on a private preview link, including on a phone, before it goes live, and every version is kept, so anything can be rolled back.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on the number of pages and what the site needs to do, so we give a fixed quote after a short call about your business. There are no plugin licences or page-builder subscriptions to pay for on top.
